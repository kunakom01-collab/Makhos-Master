This Privacy Policy describes how Makhos Master ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our") handles and protects your personal information when you download, install, and play our mobile board game application.

Summary of Core Principles:
  • We do not collect or store your personal name, physical address, or phone number.
  • Your game progress, coins, unlocked boards, and settings are saved locally on your device.
  • We integrate Google AdMob to serve advertisements (Banners, Interstitial, and Rewarded videos) which may collect advertising identifiers in accordance with Google's policies.

1. Information We Collect and Process

A. Information You Provide Directly

You are not required to create an account or provide any personally identifiable information (such as your email address, phone number, or real name) to play Makhos Master.

B. Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party Services

To support the development and free availability of the game, we integrate third-party advertising and analytics services from Google. When you use the Application, these services may automatically collect certain diagnostic and device-level data:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID/AAID), Android ID, device model, operating system version, and device language.
  • Approximate Location: Coarse location derived from IP address (used to deliver relevant ads and localized game experiences).
  • Ad Interaction Data: Ad views, clicks, video completion rates, and impression telemetry.
  • Crash & Performance Data: Crash logs and app performance statistics to help us fix bugs and improve game stability.

2. Third-Party Service Providers

3. Advertisements (Google AdMob)

Makhos Master displays advertisements to keep the game free to play. We employ three standard ad formats:

  • Banner Ads: Displayed at the bottom of menus and non-intrusive screen areas with safe margins preventing accidental clicks.
  • Interstitial Ads: Full-screen ads shown exclusively during natural breaks in gameplay (e.g., after completing a game when choosing to play again or return to the menu). We enforce a cooldown period to prevent ad spam.
  • Rewarded Video Ads: 100% voluntary, opt-in video ads that users actively choose to watch in exchange for in-game coin bonuses (e.g., 2x coins at game completion).

How to Opt Out of Personalized Advertising:

You can control and reset your Google Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time through your Android device settings:

  1. Open your device's Settings app.
  2. Navigate to Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads).
  3. Tap Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID, or toggle on Opt out of Ads Personalization.

4. Permissions Used by the Application

The Application requests only the minimal permissions required for operation:

  • android.permission.INTERNET — Required to load online AdMob advertisements and retrieve public leaderboard rankings.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Required to detect network connectivity status before requesting ads.

We do not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, storage, SMS, or precise GPS location.

5. Children's Privacy

Makhos Master is designed for general audiences and board game enthusiasts of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

In accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families policy, if you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information without your consent, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly take steps to remove such information.

6. Data Retention and Security

All gameplay data (high scores, coin balances, unlocked skins, and themes) is stored locally on your device via internal app storage. You can erase all game data at any time by clearing the application storage in your Android settings or uninstalling the app.

We value your trust in using our application and implement commercially acceptable means of protecting data processed through Google's secure APIs.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any modifications are effective immediately upon posting on this page.
